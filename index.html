Fresh things do not last forever.

On a trip longer than four days, take dried lentils, onions, garlic, tinned tomatoes, stock cubes and spices for the whole voyage. Buy fresh coriander, mint, spinach, fish and meat only for the first few days—or use frozen spinach and frozen fish instead.
